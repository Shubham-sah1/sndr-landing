How it works

From LinkedIn profile to
booked meeting in minutes

Four steps. No CSV exports. No copy-pasting. Just results.

Step 01

Find your prospects

Browse LinkedIn like you normally would. SNDR. sits in the corner, ready to capture anyone you click on — from search results to company pages.

Step 02

Discover verified emails

One click and SNDR. cross-references multiple data sources to surface a verified professional email address — no guessing, no bounces.

Step 03

Write once, personalize always

Build a sequence template with merge fields for name, company, role, and recent activity. SNDR. fills them in automatically — every email feels handwritten.

Step 04

Track every open and reply

See who opened, when, and how many times. SNDR. auto-pauses sequences the moment someone replies so you never follow up on an active conversation.
