// 01 — How it works

From LinkedIn profile to booked meeting in minutes.

01

Find your prospects

Browse LinkedIn like you normally would. SNDR. sits in the corner, capturing anyone you click on — search results, company pages, anywhere.

02

Discover verified emails

One click. SNDR. cross-references multiple data sources to surface a verified professional email. No guessing, no bounces.

Write once, personalize always

Build a sequence template with merge fields for name, company, role, and recent activity. SNDR. fills them in automatically — every email feels handwritten.
