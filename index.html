How it works

From LinkedIn profile to booked meeting in minutes.

01

Find your prospects

Browse LinkedIn normally. SNDR captures anyone you click on across search results, company pages, and profiles without any extra steps from you.

02

Discover verified emails

One click. SNDR cross-references multiple data sources to find a verified professional email address. No guessing, no bounces.

03

Write once, personalize always

Build a sequence template with merge fields for name, company, and role. SNDR fills them in automatically so every email feels handwritten.

04

Track every open and reply

See who opened, when, and how many times. SNDR auto-pauses sequences the moment someone replies so you never double-message an active thread.
