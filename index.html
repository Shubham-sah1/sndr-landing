About SNDR

SNDR is a Chrome extension and outreach platform built in India. It helps students and early professionals find verified work emails, send personalized cold emails from their own Gmail, track opens and replies on a live dashboard, and follow up automatically until they get a response. The product is live on this site and free to install.

Reach us anytime at contact@sndr.co.in or on LinkedIn.
