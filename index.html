    Questions, answered

    Didn't find yours? Write to us at contact@sndr.co.in and we'll get back to you.

    Is SNDR really free?
    Yes. Install it, connect your Gmail and start sending. No card, no trial timer.
    Which Gmail account should I use?
    Best: a Google Workspace account on your own domain. Google lets those send about 2,000 emails a day, so SNDR's 500 has plenty of room.
    Fine: a personal Gmail you've used for months.
    Avoid: a new account. Use it normally for a couple of weeks first.
    Will my Gmail get blocked?
    SNDR sends at most 500 emails a day, spaces them out, and pauses when you hit your limit. Keep emails personal and relevant, and your account stays in good standing.
    Does SNDR read my inbox?
    SNDR only looks at the email threads it started, to spot replies and bounces and stop following up. It never sends anything you didn't set up.
    Is it safe to use on LinkedIn?
    SNDR reads the names already on your screen while you scroll. It never clicks, scrolls, messages or sends connection requests for you.
    How do I install it?
    On a laptop with Chrome, click Add to Chrome, unzip the download, and load it on chrome://extensions with Developer mode on. It takes about a minute, and SNDR tells you when there's an update.

    Add SNDR to Chrome

    Free, and takes about a minute.

    1. Download SNDR and unzip the file.
    2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
    3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. Pin SNDR to your toolbar.
