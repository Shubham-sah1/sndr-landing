How it works

From LinkedIn to reply in four steps

SNDR. runs in your browser alongside your existing workflow. No new tools to learn, no data exports, no wasted hours.

Step 01
Prospect on LinkedIn
Run any LinkedIn search. SNDR. reads the results and pulls out names, job titles, and companies in one click — no manual copying.
Step 02
Find verified emails
Our AI detects company email patterns and runs SMTP verification in real time. Only real, deliverable addresses make it through.
Step 03
Launch your campaign
Write once, personalize automatically. Set your timing and delays. SNDR. sends through your own Gmail account so emails land in inboxes, not spam.
Step 04
Track and follow up
See exactly who opened your email, when, and how many times. Auto follow-ups go out when prospects don't reply — all handled without you lifting a finger.
