Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

SNDR. ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension and associated backend services handle data when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide cold email outreach automation, SNDR. processes and collects the following information:

  • Authentication Data: When you connect your Gmail account, we authenticate via Google OAuth to obtain permission to send emails and track thread replies on your behalf.
  • Contact Information: Names, emails, company names, and recruiter roles uploaded by you via CSV/Excel or scraped from professional networks (like LinkedIn) to build outreach campaigns.
  • Campaign Details: Email subjects, templates, signatures, and schedules configured by you to execute automated sequences.
  • Analytics Metadata: Email open actions, replies, and bounce events tracked via tracking pixels/links to display campaign performance metrics.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To personalize and execute automated email outreach sequences.
  • To monitor campaign statistics (sends, opens, replies, and bounces).
  • To manage follow-up sequences and pause sends if a recipient responds.
  • To maintain and improve the reliability and features of the SNDR. extension.

3. Data Storage & Security

All sensitive user configuration (such as local API preferences) is stored locally in your browser using Chrome Local Storage. Campaign lists and tracking metrics are sent securely over HTTPS and stored on our database server. We implement administrative and technical security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

4. Third-Party Sharing

SNDR. does not sell, trade, or share your data with external third parties under any circumstances. We only interact with Google's official Gmail APIs (to send emails) and AI text generation APIs (to construct email drafts if requested by you).

5. Compliance with Developer Policies

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@sndr.co.in
